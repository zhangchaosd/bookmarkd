## 中文

界面整体改版为“编辑式收藏空间”：暖纸色背景、中文衬线标题与统一的视觉语言。

- 新的登录与初始化页面、加载页、空状态、对话框和设置页。
- 首页「常看，常新」精选区展示最多 3 条置顶收藏，可在卡片上直接编辑；它们不会在下方列表中重复出现。批量操作时显示完整列表。
- 新增列表 / 卡片两种视图，按设备记住选择；卡片视图按左右半边拖放排序。
- 列表显示备注：默认行高 52px（一行备注），紧凑模式 36px 并隐藏备注。
- 配色新增默认的陶土橙；原有森林绿保留，已保存的配色保持不变，共 6 组，均支持浅色、深色与跟随系统。
- 当前页面用竖条标记，有新版本时「设置与迁移」显示「有更新」徽标。
- 无障碍：跳到内容链接、手机导航焦点限制与 Esc 关闭、文件夹菜单方向键操作、尊重“减少动态效果”设置；登录后立即应用已保存的偏好。
- 搜索无结果时提供「清除筛选」。
- 样式合并为单一样式表；浏览器测试改用 18765 等端口，避免与本机 8765 上的其他服务冲突。

升级：在「设置与迁移 → 版本与更新」中检查并安装；或下载对应平台的 `bookmarkd-*` 可执行文件，使用 `SHA256SUMS` 校验，备份数据后替换并重启。数据结构仍为 schema 1；不需要数据迁移。

此版本继续标记为预发布：尚未完成实机 Passkey 兼容性矩阵及独立安全审查。

## English

A redesigned interface — an editorial personal archive with warm paper surfaces, Chinese serif headings, and one consistent visual language.

- New login and setup pages, loading screen, empty states, dialogs, and settings.
- The “常看，常新” shelf shows up to three pinned bookmarks, editable from their cards and not repeated in the list below. Batch actions show the full list.
- List and card views, remembered per device; the card view reorders by left/right halves.
- Notes are visible in the list: rows are 52px by default (one line of notes) or 36px without notes in compact mode.
- Terracotta is the new default palette; Forest green remains and saved palettes keep their colors. Six palettes, each with light, dark, and system appearance.
- The current page is marked with a bar; available updates show a “有更新” badge on Settings.
- Accessibility: skip link, focus-trapped mobile navigation with Escape, arrow-key folder menus, and reduced-motion support; saved preferences apply immediately after login.
- Empty search results offer “清除筛选”.
- Styles are consolidated into one stylesheet; browser tests use ports such as 18765 to avoid conflicts with other local services on 8765.

Upgrade from Settings → “版本与更新”, or download the matching `bookmarkd-*` executable, verify `SHA256SUMS`, back up your data, replace the executable, and restart. Storage remains schema 1; no data migration is required.

This remains a prerelease pending physical Passkey device coverage and independent security review.
